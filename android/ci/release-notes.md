Flock Eyes: counts dairy cows and recognises each one again, on the phone.

**Install:** open this page on the phone and tap **FlockEyes.apk**. When Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (once), then tap Install. Later versions install over this one and keep your herd, counts and settings. If Play Protect says the app is unknown, tap **More details → Install anyway**.

What's in it: a **field count** (the cows in view, with each one standing clear learnt and known again), a **gate count** (cows counted each way as they cross a line, with the screen off if you like), counting the cows in a **video**, the **herd** (name or tag each cow, merge ones learnt twice), saved counts with CSV export, **backup and restore**, **debug mode**, and a speed test that finds the fastest way to run the AI on your phone.

Counting is the dependable part. Recognising is harder: it goes by the markings on a cow's side, so it needs the cow side-on, big enough in the picture and standing clear of the others; a cow's two sides look different to it until it has watched the cow turn round (or you merge the two entries); and knowing a cow again on another day is unproven. What was measured is in [docs/recognition.md](https://github.com/N-Dev/flockeyes/blob/main/docs/recognition.md).

The recognition model (MegaDescriptor) is licensed for non-commercial use only. Needs Android 8 or later on a 64-bit phone.
